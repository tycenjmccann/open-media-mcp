open-media-mcp · AWS Architecture

Any MCP agent generates video with open-weight models through seven tools on an Amazon Bedrock AgentCore Gateway. Jobs queue in SQS and render on a spot GPU in your account that exists only while there is work.

How it works
1 Agent calls a tool
  • The agent signs the MCP call (SigV4 or a JWT) to the AgentCore Gateway: create_upload for inputs, then generate_video with a prompt, backend, aspect and length.
2 Interceptor stamps the caller
  • A REQUEST interceptor Lambda writes the verified tenant and principal into the call, replacing anything the client sent.
3 Tools Lambda admits the job
  • Screens the prompt with a Bedrock Guardrail, checks the caller's quota, writes the job to DynamoDB, sends it to SQS, and raises the Auto Scaling group to one GPU per outstanding job. Returns job_id + eta_s.
4 A spot GPU pulls the job
  • The g7e worker boots with weights from S3 (Hugging Face on the very first boot), long-polls SQS, and extends its lease while it renders.
5 Render, check, publish
  • ComfyUI renders the backend's graph; Rekognition checks 6 frames; the mp4, poster and provenance manifest go to S3 and the status to DynamoDB. Flagged renders go to quarantine/.
6 Agent polls and downloads
  • get_job returns the status and presigned video_url, poster_url and manifest_url; media moves agent ⇄ S3 directly, never through the gateway. Idle workers remove themselves.
Add-ons (optional)
A Your identity provider
  • media:auth=jwt: Cognito, Okta or Entra ID tokens instead of SigV4; the tenant comes from a claim.
B Private networking
  • Private subnets behind a NAT, or your own VPC and subnets. S3 and DynamoDB gateway endpoints are always on.
C Events into your workflows
  • Every status change is an EventBridge event (Media Job Status Changed) for Step Functions, Slack or review queues.

Backends
wan2.2 (default) Apache-2.0, 720p silent T2V / I2V.
h3 (opt-in) picture + sound, lip-sync, refs; MiniMax H3 license excludes the US, EU, UK and KR.
Cost: the GPU bills only while a worker exists (spot g7e.4xlarge ≈ USD 1.3-2/h). Idle cost is the KMS key and the S3 weight cache, about USD 3/month.
